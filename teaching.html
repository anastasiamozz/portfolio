Recent News

Funding Success Supporting Innovation in Tairāwhiti.

I’m pleased to share that we have received funding from Trust Tairāwhiti to support the EIT Scholarship - Māori Economic Development.

This funding will give our students more opportunities to work on industry-relevant projects, supporting meaningful outcomes for the Tairāwhiti economy.

It’s great to see Eastern Institute of Technology Tairāwhiti students I supervise working on an Applied Project in collaboration with Eastland Port, tackling real-world challenges and applying their knowledge of embedded systems and AI in a practical industry environment.

A big thank you to our industry partners for helping create learning experiences connected with real operational needs in Tairāwhiti.

I had the privilege of organising a meeting between the Eastern Institute of Technology and LeaderBrand, a leading agricultural company in New Zealand, to explore computing internships, new collaboration opportunities, and industry support for applied research.
