I am a Senior Lecturer specialising in signal processing, embedded vision systems, video processing, and visual AI. With over 10 years in the tech industry and more than 8 years in academia, I bridge applied innovation with research excellence.

My work focuses on developing efficient vision systems for resource-constrained environments by integrating human visual perception, signal processing, and embedded system design, particularly for aerospace applications where real-time performance and bandwidth efficiency are critical. I work closely with industry partners to translate research into practical engineering solutions.

Latest News

During her visit, Hon Penny Simmonds learned about our research on embedded vision technologies for drones, which I lead in the EIT Drone Research Lab with support from Trust Tairāwhiti. The Minister highlighted the importance of strong industry connections and encouraged us to think broadly about future applications of this emerging technology.

In November 2025, I had the pleasure of presenting my research at the Image and Vision Computing New Zealand Conference (IVCNZ), co-hosted with PRICAI.

My presentation, “Visual Artificial Intelligence: Unlocking Efficiency with Psychovisual Models,” explored how human visual perception can improve AI efficiency.

In 2025, I had the privilege of speaking at the Mātai Medical Research Institute Symposium alongside an inspiring group of scientists and innovators advancing research in Aotearoa.

In December 2024, EIT lecturers Dr Anastasia Mozhaeva and Ian Allan took part in three STEM events, engaging more than 150 Tairāwhiti schoolchildren. One highlight was the Kōhine Robotics Expo, where young learners explored robotics and technology.
